We want to launch a subscription box service that rescues surplus and
cosmetically "ugly" produce from local farms and delivers it to households
in our metro area at a discount, on a recurring weekly basis. The pitch is
reducing food waste while giving people affordable, fresh produce, riding
the growing demand for sustainable food options. Our main risks are
perishable inventory, unproven demand at this scale, seasonal supply
fluctuations from farms, and competition from existing grocery delivery
incumbents.
